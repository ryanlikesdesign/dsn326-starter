Ticket — pattern

Card + List Row + Badge, composed. It sits outside the twelve and the contribution rules still apply to it. Line only, so it ignores the mode switcher and always renders density-service env-high-glare surface-line. Six fit across a 1366px pass display.

The vertical budget is the interesting part. On the real pass display the rail gets about 620px. Header and timing take ~157px and the three stacked actions take ~237px — 41% of the ticket — leaving roughly five lines for the items. A longer ticket scrolls inside that space. Ticket height is fixed and the rail must show six, so anything you add here comes out of the item list.
