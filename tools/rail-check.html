Rail conformance check

Paste your build's HTML — view-source on your published page, select all, copy. The check runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Three checks: invented Rail classes (a rail-* class that isn't in canonical Rail), inline styles (hardcoded values where a token should be), and hardcoded values in style blocks (px sizes and hex colors that bypass var(--…)). A finding is a lead, not a verdict — some are deliberate, and a deliberate one belongs in DECISIONS.md with a reason.
